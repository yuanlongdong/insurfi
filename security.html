Triple Risk Protection

Waiting Period

Deductible

10%

Monthly Limit

Maximum 5 claims per month, cumulative payout not exceeding 5 BNB, controlling extreme risk.

5 / 5 BNB

Anti-Fraud Mechanism

Multiple technical means ensure claims are real and valid, preventing various forms of fraud

Holding Snapshot Mechanism

Verify the user's INSUR holding amount at the time of the buy transaction when filing a claim, prevent temporary holding increases after loss to upgrade policy. Holding snapshots are recorded on-chain and cannot be tampered with.

Trading Pair Deduplication

Each buy transaction can only be used for one claim, marked as used after claim. Prevent the same loss transaction from being used for multiple payouts.

Buy/Sell Time Verification

Sell transaction must be later than the corresponding buy transaction, and the trading pair must match. Prevent using historical profitable transactions to impersonate loss transactions.

Flash Loan Protection

Holding must meet the 10-block time lock requirement, flash loans cannot complete the full process of holding, trading, and claiming within the same block.

Abnormal Transaction Monitoring

On-chain monitoring of abnormal transaction patterns, such as high-frequency small transactions, self-buying and self-selling, related address transactions, etc., triggering risk control warnings.

Multi-Sig Fund Management

Insurance pool funds are managed by a multi-sig wallet, large fund operations require multiple signature confirmations, preventing fund loss due to single-point private key leakage.

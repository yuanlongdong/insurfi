Long-Term Vision

InsurFi's ultimate goal is to become the insurance infrastructure in the DeFi space

Modular Insurance Middleware

Package insurance capabilities as reusable modules, other DeFi projects can integrate with one click, providing trading loss protection for their users.

Multi-Chain Ecosystem Coverage

Starting from BSC, gradually expand to mainstream public chains such as Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, providing insurance services for users across the entire ecosystem.

Institutional-Grade Insurance

Provide customized insurance solutions for institutional clients such as quantitative funds, market makers, DEX project teams, covering larger-scale trading loss risks.
